# Open Retail India

Mobile-first Tamil + English retail billing and inventory starter.

## Version 1.2
- Customer and supplier contact records
- Customer / supplier ledger entries (add due or record payment)
- Purchase entry with supplier invoice reference
- Purchase entry automatically increases product stock
- Supplier purchase balance tracking
- Existing dashboard, product catalogue, GST billing prototype, theme toggle and JSON backup retained

## Important limitations
This is a local browser prototype. Records are stored in this device/browser local storage. Export backups regularly. It does not sync between devices, authenticate users, validate GSTIN, implement full statutory GST invoice compliance or full double-entry accounting. Customer credit balances must be entered manually through the ledger; invoice creation does not automatically create customer receivables. Verify records before real business accounting.
