# Open Retail India

Smart Retail. Simple Billing. A mobile-first, Tamil + English open-source retail billing and inventory starter.

## Version 1.1
- Dashboard, product catalogue and stock tracking
- Tamil / English toggle and dark mode
- Product-level GST rate selection (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%)
- Customer name, phone and optional GSTIN on invoice
- Prototype intra-state CGST/SGST and inter-state IGST split
- Sequential local invoice numbers
- Browser print / Save as PDF invoice
- JSON backup export
- PWA manifest and service worker

## Deploy
Upload the repository files to GitHub and enable Pages from `main` / root.

## Important
This is a prototype, not certified accounting or GST-compliant invoicing software. GST rates, place of supply, HSN/SAC, seller details, invoice particulars, rounding, credit/debit notes, e-invoicing/e-way bill applicability and statutory compliance must be reviewed by a qualified tax professional before real business use. Prices are treated as exclusive of GST. Intra/inter-state is selected manually. Data remains in this browser's local storage; use backups and do not treat this as your only business record.
